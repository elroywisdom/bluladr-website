# Voice guide and sitemap

BluLadr sounds like a sharp, friendly strategist: clear and structured, with a light touch of wit. Serious about the work, never boring about it.

**Voice principles**

- **Clear first.** Short sentences. Plain words. Make the work make sense.

- **Warm, with a wink.** A dry aside is welcome ("No pressure, but you are about to start thinking better"). One per section, never forced.

- **Ask good questions.** Open with the question the reader should be asking ("Do you think so?").

- **Outcome-first.** Lead with what the client walks away with.

- **Assured, not loud.** No hype, no superlatives we can't prove.

- **British spelling.** Organisation, prioritise, scrutinise, programme.

**Brand pillars:** Clarity. Capability. Consistency.
