The eight-step approach ladder

  1. Discovery. A discovery call and questionnaire. We listen before we advise.

  2. Research. We study your market, competitors and audience.

  3. Understanding. We map where you are, what's working and what isn't.

  4. Clarity. We define the problem, the goal and the path between them.

  5. Training. We build skills in the people who will carry the work.

  6. Structure. We put frameworks, tools and workflows in place so the work holds.

  7. Partnership. We stay close through delivery. You are not left with a document and a wave.

  8. M&E. Monitoring and evaluation, so we can measure what changed.
